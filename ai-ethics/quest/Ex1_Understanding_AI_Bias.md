# Edge cases
# 1) Historical Bias: (Not just statistical unbalance)
    The data is not just “more men chose the military.” It is Women were prevented from becoming soldiers.The military historically restricted women for decades in many countries in areas of:
    - Combat bans
    - Recruitment limits
    - Promotion barriers
# 2) Measurement Bias (very important and often missed)
     Your dataset might label “soldier” based on combat roles only. 
     But modern militaries Women are more represented in these roles:
    - intelligence analysts
    - drone operators
    - cyber-warfare specialists
    - medics
    - logistics officers

# 3) Annotation (Human Labeler) Bias:
     Many datasets are manually labeled by humans.
# Example:
    An annotator sees:
    photo of woman in uniform
    They label: “nurse”
    But the same uniform on a man:
    labeled “soldier”
    This happens in real datasets (especially image datasets).
    So the bias enters before the AI ever trains.
