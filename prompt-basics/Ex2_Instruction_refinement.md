# Exercise 2: Instruction Refinement
  Objective: Convert vague requests into precise prompts.
1. Start with a vague request like: “Tell me about AI.”
2. Refine it step by step into a clear instruction, e.g.,
   - “Explain AI in simple terms for beginners.”
   - “Summarize the main AI techniques in 5 bullet points.”
3. Compare outputs and note differences.

# Prompts 1&2:
1. Tell me about AI? 2. Explain AI in simple terms for beginners step by step.” “Summarize the main AI techniques in 5 bullet points.” Compare outputs and note differences.

# Prompt 1
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that focused on building machines that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. AI is when computers are designed to think and learn like humans.

# Prompt 2
AI is like teaching computers to learn from experience rather than just following fixed instructions. Instead of a programmer writing every rule, AI systems can analyze data and identify patterns. For example, by showing an AI many pictures of cats, it learns to recognize new cat images. Once AI has learned from enough examples, it can make decisions like predicting outcomes, recommending products, or even driving cars. The more data AI gets, the better it performs, becoming smarter over time. In simple terms, AI allows computers to learn from experience instead of having every step programmed.

# AI Techniques (5 bullet points):
- Machine Learning (ML) – Algorithms that learn patterns from data.
- Deep Learning – A type of ML using neural networks with many layers.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)–Helps machines understand and generate human language.
- Computer Vision – Enables machines to interpret images and videos.
- Reinforcement Learning – AI learns by trial and error using rewards and penalties.

  # Compare outputs and note differences.
- The “Tell me about AI” response is a more formal, detailed overview with features and uses, while the “Explain AI in simple terms for beginners” response uses simpler language, analogies, and basic examples to make it easier for new learners to understand.
